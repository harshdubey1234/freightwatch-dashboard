How this dashboard works Methodology & simulation limits

Measured, not animated

All rates, baselines, flags, and evidence come from the supplied shipment and context data. Weekly rate is total freight divided by total tonne-km. History uses only the prior eight calendar weeks; peers exclude the route itself.

Geographic map, simulated movement

The India outline is derived from DataMeet’s Survey of India-based GeoJSON, simplified for display. Truck locations, speeds, counts, roads, and buildings are visual simulation elements. They are not GPS feeds, real-time shipment statuses, actual road geometries, or ETA predictions. Each scene represents your selected historical week.

Explainable policy

A candidate is ≥5% above history and either ≥15% above history or ≥20% above peers. Evidence must pass route, time, cost-effect, and magnitude checks. Applicable evidence supports a plausible explanation, not complete causal proof.

WELCOME TO FREIGHTWATCH

Your network. A new perspective.

Explore real freight findings in an illustrative 3D network.

  1. Choose a scenario to jump to a meaningful historical week.
  2. Drag the scene to rotate it. Scroll or use + / − to zoom.

  5. Inspect the evidence to see exact notes and rejected explanations.
